# Super Store Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project uses Business Intelligence (BI) and Microsoft Power BI to analyse retail sales and profitability data from the Superstore dataset. The aim is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that support evidence-based business decision-making.

## Business Objective
The objective is to evaluate sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories and regional trends. The interactive dashboard helps identify areas of strong performance, potential risks and opportunities for improvement.

## Tools and Technologies
- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** – dashboard development and data visualisation
- **Power Query** – data cleaning and preparation
- **DAX** – KPI measures and calculations
- **Kaggle Superstore Dataset** – retail sales data

## Dashboard Features
The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators (KPIs):
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Sales
- Profit Margin

It also contains:
- Sales by Region
- Profit by Customer Segment
- Sales by Category
- Sales by State map
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Total Orders by Customer Segment
- Profit by Sub-Category
- Category and Sub-Category Performance Matrix
- Interactive Region and Year slicers

## Key Findings
1. The business generated approximately **$2.30 million in sales** and **$286,397 in profit**.
2. The West region recorded the highest sales, while the South region recorded the lowest.
3. Technology generated the highest sales among the main product categories.
4. The Consumer segment contributed approximately 46.8% of total profit.
5. Tables and Bookcases recorded negative profit, highlighting the need for further investigation.
6. Monthly sales fluctuated over the period covered by the dataset, making trend monitoring important.

## Business Recommendations
- Review the pricing, discounting and costs associated with loss-making sub-categories.
- Investigate regional sales differences and identify ways to improve performance in the South.
- Maintain appropriate stock levels for high-performing products.
- Develop customer retention and cross-selling strategies.
- Monitor monthly sales trends to improve inventory planning and forecasting.
- Use the dashboard regularly to monitor KPIs and evaluate business performance.
